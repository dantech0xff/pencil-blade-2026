# Static Reconstruction Unknowns

Unknowns are never filled with convenient behavior. The versioned, machine-readable authority
is [`fidelity/residual-gap-ledger.json`](./fidelity/residual-gap-ledger.json), generated from
[`reference/fidelity-metric-v1.json`](../reference/fidelity-metric-v1.json). It records every
current inference, unknown, compatibility/safety exception, declared platform divergence,
rights decision, custody blocker, and release blocker together with its score effect.
