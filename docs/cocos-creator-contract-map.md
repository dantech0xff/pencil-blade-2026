  rendering, audio, persistence, Physics2D, and scene lifecycle.
- Creator has imported the exact recovered APK asset corpus into the `game` bundle and
  serialized one persistent Canvas component map for Main Menu, Mode Select, Classic, and Crazy.
- The Classic route owns its exact normal-fruit, cut-half, critical-particle, score-HUD,
  fail-marker, BasicBlade, audio, and mode-0 result consumers. The Crazy route adds the recovered
  60-second controller graph, standard-bomb fuse/explosion, special/electric/magnet/Dragon
  entities, objectives, pause, audio, and mode-1 result consumers.
- Mode Select preserves its exact 45-particle/225-draw unlock burst. Modes `0` and `1` use
  separately prepared production owners; modes `2` through `5` remain fail closed.
- Fresh Creator-served Browser Preview passes after the Dragon late-hit splash
  `UIOpacity`-after-destroy crash fix. It confirms production entry and
  Pause/Resume/Replay/Quit through the transactional shell with zero errors; the isolated
  temporary storage profile is deleted after the run.

  the inventory workflow passes `14/14`; policy positive plus `4/4` negative fixtures and
  Creator strict TypeScript pass; independent review reports no P0/P1.

  leaderboard, audio flags, network sentinel, and rated state plus separate Mode Select unlock
  keys; other Settings fields plus first-launch and Main Menu exit-save behavior remain open.
